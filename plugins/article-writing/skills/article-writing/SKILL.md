# Writing Articles

When the user is drafting, structuring, or revising an article — pitch or long-form — follow this guide.

## The 300-word pitch format

When the user asks for a pitch:

```
Title: <provocative but accurate, no clickbait, ideally 6-10 words>

Hook (1-2 sentences): A concrete observation, contradiction, or stake. Reader knows immediately whether to read on.

Topics (3 bullets): Each is the kernel of a section. Specific, not generic.
- <topic 1>
- <topic 2>
- <topic 3>

Why now (1 sentence): What recent event, shift, or release makes this worth writing this week, not next quarter.
```

Word count target: 250–300. Trim mercilessly.

## Voice rules

- **No AI tells.** Strip "in conclusion", "moreover", "delve into", "tapestry", "landscape", em-dashes-as-pause, "It's important to note that".
- **Write like you talk.** Contractions, sentence fragments where they earn it, occasional one-line paragraphs.
- **Specific over abstract.** "Cursor 0.50's tab-tab autocomplete" beats "modern AI IDE features."
- **Show stakes.** Who wins, who loses, what changes for the reader.
- **Cut every sentence that could be said about anything else.**

## Structure rules

- **Title and hook do 70% of the work.** If the reader doesn't get past them, nothing else matters.
- **No throat-clearing.** First paragraph carries weight, not "this article will discuss…".
- **One idea per section.** If you can't summarize a section in one line, it has two ideas.
- **Conclusion is the strongest paragraph, not the weakest.** End on a sharp observation or open question, not "thus we see that…".

## Critique dimensions

When critiquing a draft, score each on 1–5 and give line-level edits:

1. **Voice / non-AI feel** — would a sharp reader peg this as AI-written?
2. **Hook strength** — does the first 2 sentences earn the next 200?
3. **Specificity** — concrete examples and named things vs. abstractions?
4. **Structure** — one idea per section? Logical flow?
5. **Conclusion** — does it land or fizzle?
6. **Length / tightness** — what could be cut without loss?

For each dimension under 4, name specific lines and propose alternatives.
